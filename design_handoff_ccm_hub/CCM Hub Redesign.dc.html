4 · Mental health impacts of recurrent flooding

The 2022 floods displaced over 1.4 million people across the Niger Delta, with prolonged displacement emerging as a primary driver of psychological distress among affected households.

Respondents reported elevated rates of anxiety, grief, and sleep disruption, particularly among women heads of household and young people who had lost schooling.2

Community-based support structures — including faith groups and farmer cooperatives — were identified as key sources of resilience and informal care.1

These findings align with regional dialogue testimony and point to the need for mental-health-sensitive disaster response protocols.
